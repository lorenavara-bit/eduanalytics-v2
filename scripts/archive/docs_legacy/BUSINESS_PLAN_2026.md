# Business Plan: EduAnalytics v2 (EdTech Startup)
**Target Market:** Spain / European Union
**Cycle:** Q1 2026

## 1. Executive Summary
EduAnalytics v2 is an AI-powered educational platform designed to close the gap between standard textbook curricula (SM, Santillana, Anaya, etc.) and personalized student needs. By leveraging real-time data from official sources (INTEF) and proprietary AI models, we provide parents and educators with hyper-personalized learning materials, early neuro-divergence detection, and adaptive study roadmaps.

## 2. Value Proposition (USP)
*   **Curriculum-Aware AI:** Unlike generic AI (ChatGPT), our system understands the specific LOMLOE curriculum and specific textbook structures used in Spanish schools.
*   **Learning Style Personalization:** Automatic adjustment of content based on VARK/Felder-Silverman models validated through interactive quizzes.
*   **Parent-Centric Data:** Moving away from "grades only" to "insight-driven" education management.
